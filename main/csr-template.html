III. CASE DEVELOPMENT

The family was identified as poor under the Listahanan and was enrolled in the Pantawid Pamilyang Pilipino Program (4Ps) in 2022. For over three years in the 4Ps program, Megan, the grantee, has consistently complied with the program's conditions, regularly attending Family Development Sessions (FDS), participating in health monitoring, and ensuring the children's school attendance. Despite economic challenges, the family has strived to meet their basic needs with the support from the 4Ps program. According to the Social Welfare and Development Indicator (SWDI) assessment conducted on May 23, 2024, the family had reached the Self-Sufficient level of well-being. This was largely attributed to the mother, Lucia, who was working abroad as a Domestic Helper in Hong Kong, earning approximately P20,000.00 per month. Her husband, Benjamin, also contributed to the household income through farming, earning about P7,000.00 monthly.

However, recent events have caused a significant setback in the family's economic stability. Lucia returned home in April 2025 after her overseas contract ended. While waiting for a new opportunity abroad, she is currently engaged in food vending in their community, with a modest income of P3,000.00. Her return was also partly influenced by a serious family conflict. Early in 2025, while Lucia was still abroad, Benjamin left home to live with another woman. Although he continues to send sacks of rice for the family's consumption, his financial support has become inconsistent and significantly reduced.

Meanwhile, Megan, the program grantee, is currently pregnant with her second child and stays at home to care for her eldest son. Her common-law partner, Ronald, works as a laborer, earning approximately P4,000.00 per month. Despite the challenges, all monitored children remain in school. The previously monitored child, Jorell, is now a first-year college student at Trinidad Municipal College. Jezrel is in Grade 11 at Pilar Technical Vocational High School, and Leslie is in Grade 4 at Cagawasan Elementary School.

The most recent SWDI assessment, conducted on June 17, 2025, shows that the family's well-being status has declined to Level 2 or Subsistence. This downgrade reflects the economic shock experienced due to multiple factors. First, the loss of Lucia's stable overseas income; second, the reduced financial support from Benjamin; lastly, and the increasing financial demands of supporting children in school, covering expenses such as food, school supplies, boarding house rentals, and other educational needs. At present, the family is in a vulnerable state and may benefit from case management intervention, livelihood support, and referrals to appropriate social welfare services to help them regain economic stability and improve their overall well-being.
