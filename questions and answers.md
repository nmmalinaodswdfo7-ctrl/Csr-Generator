# CSR and SCSR Public Speaking Guide

## 1. Main Message (What this app is)
Use this line:

"This application helps us create CSR and SCSR reports faster, with fewer manual errors, and with step-by-step guidance until PDF export."

Use this line for audience:

"Think of this as one workspace where we login, choose a household, complete guided sections, preview, and export the final report."

---

## 2. 60-Second Overview Script
Use this exact flow while speaking:

1. "First, we login using ID and municipality."
2. "Second, we select the correct household from the card list."
3. "Third, we choose the workflow: CSR or SCSR."
4. "Fourth, we complete each required section step by step."
5. "Fifth, we run Print Preview to check layout."
6. "Finally, we export the PDF to Desktop under Social Case Report."

Closing line:

"So the app is not just encoding; it is a guided workflow from data selection to final printable report."

---

## 3. How To Explain Each Screen (Casual User Style)

## Login Screen
What to say:

"Enter your ID, choose your municipality, then click Login. The app validates your ID and loads your municipality data."

If asked about Restore Session:

"Restore Session is for quickly reopening if your required login files already exist."

## Household List Screen
What to say:

"This is our household selection area. You can search by HHID or name, filter by barangay or status, then click Create on the correct card."

About Update Data:

"Update Data refreshes the municipality dataset. Use this when new source data is available."

## Workflow Picker (CSR vs SCSR)
What to say:

"After clicking Create, choose if you are doing CSR or SCSR. The app opens the right form steps for that workflow."

## Stepper Area
What to say:

"The stepper shows your current progress. You can move step by step and go back anytime."

## Print Preview and Export
What to say:

"Print Preview is for checking layout only. Export is the one that creates the official PDF file."

---

## 6. How To Answer User Questions Clearly (Simple Formula)
Use this 4-part answer style:

1. "What it is"
2. "Where to click"
3. "What happens"
4. "What to do next"

Example:

Q: "What does Update Data do?"
A:
"It refreshes municipality records. Click Update Data on the household list toolbar. The cards reload with latest data. After that, continue selecting household and proceed."

---

## 7. Most Likely Questions and Ready Answers

Q1: "Can I create both CSR and SCSR for the same household?"
A1: "Yes. They are separate workflows and saved separately."

Q2: "Why can’t I export yet?"
A2: "One or more required sections are incomplete. Follow the red-highlighted step and complete missing fields."

Q3: "Is Print Preview already the final file?"
A3: "No. Preview is only visual checking. Click Export to generate the PDF."

Q4: "Where is the exported file?"
A4: "On Desktop, inside Social Case Report folder. CSR and SCSR go to separate subfolders."

Q5: "Do I need to click Save every time?"
A5: "Most sections auto-save. You can still watch the save status text for confirmation."

Q6: "What is Restore Session?"
A6: "It reopens your session using existing MLS and municipality files."

Q7: "What if I deleted a family member by mistake?"
A7: "Open Restore Member and bring that member back."

Q8: "Why is Per Capita Income not editable in SCSR?"
A8: "It is auto-computed by the app to keep values consistent."

Q9: "Why did existing records open instead of creating a new one?"
A9: "The app avoids duplicate records per household and workflow."

Q10: "What does Save beside signatory names do?"
A10: "It stores default names for the municipality so future records auto-use them."

---

## 8. Most Confusing Points (How to Explain Simply)

Confusion: "Restore Member Data vs Restore Member"
Answer:
"Restore Member Data resets edited values. Restore Member brings back deleted rows."

Confusion: "Update Data button appears/disappears"
Answer:
"It appears when source changes are detected or cleanup is pending."

Confusion: "CSR and SCSR look different"
Answer:
"Correct, because each workflow has different required sections and output format."

Confusion: "Why export blocks even if most fields are done"
Answer:
"Export checks all required sections, not just current step."

- "Preview first, export second."

## 10. Suggested Demo Flow for Public Presentation
1. Login.
2. Show search/filter on household list.
3. Click Create and choose CSR.
4. Fill sample values in each CSR step quickly.
5. Show validation by leaving one required field empty, then fix it.
6. Open Print Preview.
7. Export PDF.
8. Go back, choose SCSR, show tabbed background section and export path difference.

Closing line:

"The app standardizes reporting, reduces missed fields, and makes final report generation faster and clearer for staff."
